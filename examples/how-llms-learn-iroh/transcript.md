# How LLMs learn, taught to the viewer through one running example: 'the tea is getting... cold'

[  0.0s] s1 TITLE 第一課 猜字 / First Lesson: Guessing the Next Word


[  4.6s] s2
Finish this for me. The tea is getting... cold. You knew. The machine knows too, and that is its only skill: the next word.

[ 19.7s] s3
Inside it, every word is a place on a table. Tea sits near cup, and far from sword. Closeness is meaning. Nothing more.

[ 34.8s] s4
Pour water through many strainers. Each layer keeps a little and passes the rest. One cup at the bottom: the word that scored highest.

[ 49.9s] s5
It guessed warm. The scroll said cold. That gap is the lesson. Each one nudges the guess a little closer. A trillion nudges, and it knows.

[ 65.0s] s6 TITLE 戒 / The Caution


[ 69.6s] s7
Nobody told it what is true. Only what was written. Write a lie ten thousand times, and it will say the lie warmly.

[ 84.7s] s8
So ask it what people usually say. Do not ask it what is so. Now. Your tea really is getting cold.
