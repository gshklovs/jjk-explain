# /explain-hxh

Turn `$ARGUMENTS` into a freeze-frame narrator lecture video. The renderer is shared with `/explain`: `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/../explain/scripts/render.py`. The only switch is `"style": "hxh"` in script.json.

## 1. Load the bible
Read `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/reference.md` (what the power explanations feel like, beats, registers, tiers, cast, style lock, diagram presets, title card and sound vocabulary). Do not skip it; it is the whole voice. Do not load the JJK or Iroh bibles; the three voices must not blend.

## 2. Understand the concept
If the concept refers to the current conversation or codebase, gather what you need first. Reduce it to: the rule (one sentence, like law), the demonstration (a physical thing a character does that can be frozen and diagrammed), the condition (what must hold, what it costs), the one number, the exception ("however"), and the consequence (why the world uses it; the takeaway). Pick a tier unless `--tier` is given. Roll the "temperature": vary tier, register, the character, the setting and the diagram preset; never reuse the previous run's character or diagram. Ask: where is the restriction that is the source of the strength?

Object references: if the concept is or contains a specific complicated object (a drive, a gripper, an engine, a specific character, vehicle or product), fetch 2-3 reference images of it first via a research subagent and pass them as `"objects"` (see the bible's "Object references" section). The model will not get a complicated shape right from words alone.

## 3. Write `script.json`
Workspace root: `python3 ${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/../explain/scripts/render.py --where` (the repo, or `$EXPLAIN_HOME`). Path: `<workspace>/out/<slug>-hxh/script.json` (slug = kebab of the concept; the `-hxh` suffix keeps it apart from other takes on the same concept). Shape and rules are in reference.md. Mandatory: `"style": "hxh"` at the top level. Target 60-75 s: 115-145 words, 6-8 scenes, 1-2 title cards. Title-card `kanji` is Japanese, two space-separated groups (category, then name). Every shot gets a `video_prompt` that stands alone (the renderer prepends the style lock) and a `sound` line from the bible's vocabulary. Exactly one concrete number in the whole script; the diagram overlay in the demonstration shot physically IS the mechanism (a ring of pins, a hexagon, a chain, a countdown ring), not decoration.
Hard rule (see "Narration is not stage direction" in reference.md): narration states the rule, the condition, the cost, the number and the consequence, or addresses the viewer as the student; it never describes what the camera shows. Test every line: if it could be pasted into `video_prompt` unchanged ("he turns the cam", "the disc wobbles", "the frame freezes"), cut it or turn it into a law ("A cam offset from center cannot spin the disc; it can only make it orbit").

Off-screen speakers: a tagged speaker with no body on screen (an AI, a radio, a narrator nobody should mouth) goes in `"offscreen": ["tag"]` (style default or script level); its scenes render silent and the line comes from that tag's fish.audio voice, so no mouth can be animated. See the bible's "Off-screen speakers" section.

## 4. Render
```
python3 ${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/../explain/scripts/render.py <workspace>/out/<slug>-hxh/script.json --voice fish
```
Flags are the same as `/explain`: `--dry-run` (placeholder clips, no fal spend; add `EXPLAIN_LEAN_DRY=1` to the environment to see the assembled lean prompts), `--music /path.mp3` (default for this style: first audio file in `<workspace>/assets/hxh/`, else `assets/`, else `$EXPLAIN_MUSIC`), `--resolution 480P|768P`, `--no-captions`, `--narration FILE`, `--voice fish` (only used off lean mode; see below).
Model: every shot in this style uses the reference stills and the narrator voice sample (lean mode), so it is routed to `h3-max` reference-to-video ($0.08/s at either resolution; 480P saves nothing). A 7-scene lesson with 6 shots is about $6-7. Title cards are local and free. `--model` / `--resolution` / a top-level `"model"` / `"resolution"` / `EXPLAIN_MODEL` / `EXPLAIN_RESOLUTION` still apply, but only matter once you opt out of refs and the sample (`"refs": []` + `"voice_sample": null` puts a shot back on turbo, text-to-video, Fish narration). Reference-to-video is $0.05/s at 480P and $0.08/s at 768P, so it follows `--resolution` (480P default); `EXPLAIN_REF_RESOLUTION` forces one for reference scenes alone.
Keys: the renderer reads `<workspace>/.env` (see `.env.example`) and the environment. If they live only in the user's shell rc, load them without printing: `eval "$(grep -E "^\s*export (FAL_API_KEY|FISH_AI_API_KEY|OPENAI_API_KEY)=" ~/.zshrc)"`. Only `FAL_API_KEY` is needed for the default (lean) path. If it is missing, run with `--dry-run` and tell the user.

### Lean mode (the default): the model speaks the narration, off-screen
The style carries `voice_sample: "assets/ref/hxh/narrator-voice.wav"` (13.9 s of the narrator clone reading two calm sentences, mono, no music). With no `lipsync` key in the script, the renderer skips TTS entirely: each shot's narration is written into the prompt and the sample goes in as `reference_audio_urls`. The key difference from `/explain-iroh` and `/explain-rick`: the narrator has no body. The renderer's prompt for this style ends with "The characters on screen do not speak and keep their mouths closed. An unseen narrator says, in the voice of Audio 1, exactly these words and nothing else: ..." (style fields `lean_tag: "narrator"` + `say_lines["narrator"]` in `render.py`: untagged narration is treated as the speaker "narrator", the same per-speaker mechanism the stark style uses for its bodiless AI), so the students stay silent and the voice is a voice-over. Verified 2026-09-02 on the cycloidal lesson: the clips' own tracks carried the narration word for word (Whisper transcripts matched) and in the face shots (s2, s6, s7) both boys kept their mouths closed. Scene length is estimated from word count (`words_per_sec` 2.0 for this style, plus 1.0 s per sentence pause, capped at 14 s), so keep each shot at or under 22 words; a 24-word shot still renders but hits the cap. The character's release line in beat 8 is read by the narrator like everything else. Set `"voice_sample": null` or `"lipsync": true` to go back to Fish narration (keys below).
Music in lean mode: the model writes its own ambience and score from the `sound` line, and that track is kept at full level; the style's music bed is still mixed under it at -15 dB with sidechain ducking keyed by the clip audio (so it ducks under the model's voice and score alike). Keep `sound` lines to ambience; ask for "no music" in `sound` if the two scores fight.

### Reference stills (the likeness path)
The style's default `refs` (`assets/ref/hxh/gon-bust.jpg`, `gon-3q.jpg`, `gon-full.jpg`, `killua-bust.jpg`, `killua-3q.jpg`, `killua-full.jpg`; single-character 16:9 crops cut from the show's Nen-training scenes) with `ref_owners` (`gon ×3, killua ×3`) and `ref_labels` route every shot to reference-to-video; the renderer prepends "Image 1 to Image 3 show the boy in green; Image 4 to Image 6 show the white-haired boy; keep each character's face, hair and clothes consistent with their images". Because the refs are these two, the `cast` paragraph should describe one of them (the student in green: "a wiry teenage boy with spiky dark hair, bright wide eyes, a sleeveless green jacket over a black shirt, green shorts and boots"; the white-haired boy: "a slim boy with messy white hair, sharp blue eyes, a dark long-sleeved turtleneck and lilac trousers, hands in pockets"), and prompts should say "the boy in green" / "the white-haired boy" when both appear. Missing default stills are skipped with a warning. Default `seed` 1128 keeps the look stable; override with a top-level `"seed"`. Per-script `"refs"` + `"ref_owners"` (parallel lists) replace the defaults; `"refs": []` opts out (text-only shot on turbo). `assets/ref/hxh/` also holds Wing (`wing-bust.jpg`, `wing-bust-2.jpg`, `wing-forest.jpg`) if a shot needs the teacher (add them per script with owner `wing` and a `ref_labels` entry), set frames (`set-hotel-room.jpg`, `set-arena.jpg`, `set-canyon.jpg`) and two-shots for your own reference when writing prompts, not for `refs`. Inventory in `docs/hxh-research.md`; everything under `assets/` is gitignored.

### Music (beds in `assets/hxh/`, pick per lesson)
Set `"music"` at the top level of script.json (bare name, substring match against `assets/hxh/`, then `assets/`):
- `"lecture"` (file `lecture-latent-power.mp3`, "Latent Power", the cue under the four-principles lecture in the show; patient piano and strings): the default for `hxh-lecture`; sits under narration without pulling focus.
- `"ominous"` (`ominous-kingdom-of-predators.mp3`, "Kingdom of Predators", the dark arc's theme; low choir, slow build): for `hxh-ominous`, when the narrator knows how this ends.
- `"rules"` (`rules-understanding.mp3`, "Understanding"; calm, curious, a mechanical pulse): for `hxh-rules`, itemized game-rules exposition.
Always set one; the filenames are prefixed so the alphabetical fallback is the lecture bed.
`--music FILE` on the command line overrides; with neither, the first file alphabetically in `assets/hxh/` is used. The beds are gitignored; `docs/hxh-research.md` has the track names and the `yt-dlp` recipe to refetch them.

### Fish narration (alternative)
`--voice fish` with `"voice_sample": null` or `"lipsync": true` in script.json uses the fish.audio voice in `STYLES["hxh"]["fish_voice"]` in `render.py` ("Hunter x Hunter Narrator", the public English-dub narrator clone, id `07f821df8a8e4eb7af871d19de5c4619`, ~29k generations; fallback "Hxh Narrator" `8b7f89d0bed044df85e04c6a877e845e`). Override with `HXH_FISH_VOICE_ID=<model id>`; needs `FISH_AI_API_KEY`. Speed is always 1.0; pacing comes from the 1.0 s sentence pauses and the scene chunking. The lean-mode sample was generated from this same voice, so both paths sound like the same narrator. Write `narrator_voice` for a calm, precise, unhurried reader who leaves a beat before every "however" (lean mode ignores it; the sample sets the delivery).

## 5. Deliver
Print the script scene by scene, the final path, then open the mp4. QuickTime shows a blank document if the same path was re-rendered while open, so quit it first: `osascript -e 'tell application "QuickTime Player" to quit'; open -a "QuickTime Player" <path>`. Report cost from the renderer's summary line.
